Privacy Policy

Reelflow Publisher Hub · Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. What we collect

  • TikTok account data, via TikTok Login: your TikTok open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, needed to publish on your behalf (scope video.publish / video.upload).
  • Content you submit: the video files, captions and posting settings you choose, and their publishing status.

2. How we use it

Only to provide the Service: showing which account you are posting to, uploading the videos you choose to TikTok, scheduling posts, and showing their status. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties except TikTok as required to publish your content.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and post records are stored on the Service's server and protected from public access. Video files are used only for the upload you request.

4. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time, and revoke access in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Apps. On request we delete your stored tokens, post records and uploaded files. Contact us at the address below.
